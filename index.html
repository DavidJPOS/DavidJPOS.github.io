ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR · STATISTICS & DATA ANALYTICS

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Limerick, and a member of the Mathematics Applications Consortium for Science and Industry (MACSI).

My research focuses on the science of social networks. I develop analytical tools to understand how network structure affects the spread of information and behaviour — for example, whether fake news spreads differently than a virus — the social mechanisms behind the emergence of behaviours on networks, and how online networks shape and reflect public deliberation, such as the polarised opinion groups that formed around Irish referendums on Twitter/X. I am also keenly interested in applying statistical methods to real-world industry problems.

Interests

  • Social network analysis & community detection
  • Complex contagion and information diffusion
  • Branching process models on networks
  • Computational social science
  • Applied statistics for industry
